## 📋 About This Game

Romestead places you in an open world where you build a home from the ground up. You manage resources, craft tools, and protect your land. The game supports solo play and multiplayer cooperative modes. You grow crops, mine stone, and refine materials like olive oil or granite. The world reacts to your choices. Your goal involves turning a wild plot of earth into a thriving town.

## 🎮 Gameplay Basics

You start the game with basic tools and a small plot of land. Gather wood and stone to build your first structure. Use the clay you find near the river to craft pots and building supplies. Trade your refined goods like olive oil with neighbors to get new items.

Multiplayer sessions allow your friends to join your world. You share resources and work on large building projects together. Keep your home safe from weather events by reinforcing walls and creating heat sources.

## 🛠 Troubleshooting Common Issues

If the game does not start or runs slowly, try these solutions.

*   Update your graphics drivers: Visit the website for your card manufacturer and download the latest controller software.
*   Check your file storage: Clear extra files from your hard drive to ensure the game has enough space to save your progress.
*   Adjust graphic settings: Open the game menu and lower the resolution or texture quality if the frame rate feels choppy.
*   Restart your computer: A fresh system boot often fixes background errors that interfere with software performance.
*   Verify permissions: Run the application as an administrator if the game fails to save your configurations.
